# Nigeria-out-of-school-primary-children-analysis
# 📊 Analysis of Out-of-School Primary School Children in Nigeria by Region

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes the distribution of out-of-school primary school children across Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). The dashboard provides insights into regional disparities, state-level trends, school-age population, enrolment, and Net Enrolment Rate (NER), helping identify areas that require targeted educational interventions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of out-of-school primary school children across Nigeria.
- Compare out-of-school children across the six geopolitical regions.
- Identify states with the highest number of out-of-school children.
- Examine school-age population and enrolment figures.
- Assess Net Enrolment Rate (NER) and each state's contribution to the national out-of-school population.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- A total of **10.65 million** primary school children are out of school across Nigeria.
- The **South South** region records the highest number of out-of-school children.
- **Rivers State** has the highest number of out-of-school children, with approximately **922,217**.
- Significant disparities exist between states, indicating unequal access to primary education.
- States classified as **High** and **Very High Risk** account for a substantial proportion of Nigeria's out-of-school children.
- The dashboard enables users to filter results by region, state, and risk level for deeper analysis.
